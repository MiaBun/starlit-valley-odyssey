# General Information about Emerald

Emeralds are minerals that are used primarily as the currency for trading with villagers and wandering traders.

## Where to find

Emerald ore generates in mountains and windswept hills biomes in the form of ore features, and attempts to generate 100 times per chunk in blobs of 0–4 ores. Emerald ore can be exposed to the sky.

### Newly added properties

Can be sold for: 32 coins
