# General Information about Block of Diamond

A block of diamond, known internally as a diamond block, is a precious mineral block equivalent to nine diamonds.

## Where to find

Blocks of diamond generate as the cores of "obsidian rooms" and "lava rooms" in woodland mansions

### Newly added properties

Can be sold for: 2304 coins
