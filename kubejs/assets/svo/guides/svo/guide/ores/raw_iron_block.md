# General Information about Block of Raw Iron

A block of raw iron, known internally as a raw iron block, is a raw metal block equivalent to nine raw iron.

## Where to find

Blocks of raw iron can be found rarely inside of huge ore veins below Y=0, with a 2% chance of replacing any given iron ore.

### Newly added properties

Can be sold for: 72 coins
