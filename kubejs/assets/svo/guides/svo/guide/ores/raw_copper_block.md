# General Information about Block of Raw Copper

A block of raw copper, internally known as a raw copper block, is a raw metal block equivalent to nine raw copper.

## Where to find

Blocks of raw copper can be found rarely inside of copper ore veins above Y=0, with a 2% chance of replacing any given copper ore. They cannot replace copper ore in regular ore blobs.

### Newly added properties

Can be sold for: 36 coins
