# Sweet Berries

Sweet berries are a food item obtained from sweet berry bushes and are used to plant them.

Sweet berry bushes are quick-growing plants that grow sweet berries. Players and most mobs are slowed down and take constant damage while moving through them. They can be found naturally in taiga and snowy taiga biomes.
