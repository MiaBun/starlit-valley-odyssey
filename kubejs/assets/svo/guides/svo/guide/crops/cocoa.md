# Cocoa Beans

Cocoa beans are items obtained from cocoa pods, and are used to plant more of them as well as to craft brown dye and cookies.

Cocoa pods are plant blocks that can only be placed on jungle log sides and grow cocoa beans. They can be found naturally in jungles.
