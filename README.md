<h1 align="center">Starlit Valley: Odyssey</h1>
<p align="center"><b><i>A forge 1.20.1 modpack that aims to recreate Stardew Valley in minecraft in it's own unique way.</i></b></p>

## what is this modpack about?

Starlit Valley: Odyssey is a modpack that aims to recreate Stardew Valley inside of minecraft in it's own way, it'll combine elements of Overcooked, Plate Up!, Diner Dash, Story of Seasons: A wonderful Life and Animal crossing: New Horizons.
this all whilst also expanding upon Stardew Valley's progression to include Create, Gregtech, Blood Magic, Botania, Ars Nouveau and Norse Mythology (and a sprinkle of moomins).

## what minecraft version?

this modpack will be on forge 1.20.1

## will there ever be a version for newer minecraft versions?

Currently there are no plans of updating to 1.21.1, this is mostly due to a lot of our coremods not being updated yet.

## will there be changes to vanilla mechanics?

Yes there will be some major changes to accomplish this goal, however each and every change will be fully detailed.

## what about overwhelm?

In a effort to avoid overwhelm this modpack will provide a fully detailed Quest Book, GuideME in-game wiki, Field Guide and it'll try to avoid common modding thropes like ore spam in the overworld, alongside this it'll also utilise EMI++ Stack Groups to stack items together into foldable categories.
