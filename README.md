<h1 align="center">Starlit Valley: Odyssey</h1>
<p align="center"><b><i>A forge 1.20.1 modpack that has a detailed farming, magic and tech progression</i></b></p>

## what is this modpack about?

Starlit Valley: Odyssey will be a modpack all about combining stardew valley, minecraft, create above & beyond, gregtech new horizons, gregtech odyssey, monifactory, nomifactory, omnifactory and star technology, this will be done in a guided way, there will be 6 chapters, each focussing on introducing new mechanics, the first 4 will be purely focussed on introducing farming mechanics, setting up your farm and introducing the nether and mining dimension whilst chapter 5 will be focussed on magic  in a school system, chapter 6 will be focussed on introducing tech and will be the ultimate endgame, the main focus is to make a big modpack that'll take a while to complete whilst not feeling overwhelming since a lot of modpack lack this or are either farming, tech or magic based.

why not play any of the above mentioned modpacks?
well it's quite simple, in the friend group we all want something different.. some want stardew valley like fishing, farming etc, some want magic and others want tech using create & gregtech... whilst we could go for a kitchen sink like All the Mods, these modpcaks tend to be overwhelming... this is why i decided to make a custom modpack that combines stardew valley with a late game progression line into magic and a endgame progression line into create & gregtech whilst making it as guided as possible.

## what minecraft version?

this modpack will be on forge 1.20.1

## will there ever be a version for newer minecraft versions?

for the time being? no, some of the main mods in the modpack are not yet on neoforge 1.21.1, we'll have to wait for these, however once these are updated the pack will 100% update to 1.21.1

## will there be changes to vanilla mechanics?

there are a few changes.
- tool progression will be revamped to include copper and gold tools
- the nether and end will be unlocked at different stages and the way to access these 2 dimensions will be slightly different compared to vanilla
- the enchantment table will be unlocked later on (during chapter 5), however don't be afraid, enchanted books will be obtainable for the most important enchants before chapter 5, the enchantment table will mostly act as a way to obtain higher levels of enchants :)
- livestock (sheeps, cows, pigs, chickens) will actually require love and attention, you'll need to feed them food & water, you'll need to brush them etc this to make them more engaging 
- farmland won't be watered by water source blocks, instead you'll use a water can or sprinkler to water crops on a daily base, this to make crops more engaging
- crops won't be able to be speed accelerated with bonemeal, instead watering a crop will automatically grow it on a daily base

## **what about overwhelm?

the main focus on this modpack will be to slowly introduce new mechanics and carefully explain them, the changes that will be made to vanilla mechanics will also be explained beforehand, the quest book will be extremely guided and will be accompanied by both create's ponder system and ae2's guideME system to explain mechanics, machines, multiblocks etc in a intuitive way, to avoid ore clutter there will be a mining dimension with layers, each layer having their own ore, whilst mobs from mods will be sorted through and have the spawn rules changed to avoid overwhelming the overworld, nether and end, loot will also be changed to be less cluttered and more useful, this all to make the modpack feel as satisfying as possible whilst not having any overwhelm that may normally be introduced by mods
